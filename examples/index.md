# Examples · Films made from JSON documents · ultrafast

> Films made with ultrafast, each with the JSON document that made it: NASA footage cut to its own sound, a podcast clip, data stories, 3D launch films, a simulation and title sequences.

URL: https://ultrafast.mov/examples/

Examples

# Every one comes with its document.

12 films, each one JSON document, made by a coding agent. Open any of them to see how it was made.

Real footage

## Cut, captioned, graded.

NASA footage and sound, edited by documents an agent wrote and `review` passed.

[Video: Two launches, one count](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/liftoff.mp4)

### Two launches, one count

Apollo 11 and Artemis I lift off in alternating shots, cut to the 1969 launch commentary, with each number in large type and both launches' clocks counting down to zero.

2.39:1 · 1920×804 · 22.5 s · exported in 3.36 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/liftoff.json>)

[Video: Podcast clip](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/podcast.mp4)

### Podcast clip

A vertical clip of NASA astronaut Mark Vande Hei aboard the space station, with word-timed captions, a waveform driven by his voice, and Earth cut in on the words that name it.

9:16 · 1080×1920 · 26 s · exported in 6.00 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/podcast.json>)

## Nothing here was filmed.

The 3D, the simulations and the type are rendered by ultrafast from each document, every frame. Nothing here was filmed or made in another app.

[Video: Pottery class teaser](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/clay.mp4)

3D rendered by ultrafast

### Pottery class teaser

A lump of wet terracotta is centred, opened and pulled into a vase on a turning wheel under window light, beside the class title and its details.

9:16 · 1080×1920 · 10 s · exported in 1.68 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/clay.json>)

[Video: Lens launch](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/product.mp4) 3D rendered by ultrafast

### Lens launch

A camera lens that doesn't exist, lit like a studio product shot: light finds the knurled barrel, the focus scale turns, eleven blades stop down and the optics slide apart under callouts.

16:9 · 1920×1080 · 16 s · exported in 4.88 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/product.json>)

[Video: Celadon dish](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/bloom.mp4)

### Celadon dish

A carving grows across a celadon dish by reaction–diffusion, stepped every frame; the camera closes in on the glaze, then molten glaze floods back over it.

1:1 · 1080×1080 · 15 s · exported in 1.06 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/bloom.json>)

[Video: Concert poster](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/swiss.mp4)

### Concert poster

A Swiss-style poster for a night of four concerts. Its dial is the score of its own track, and the headline widens and narrows on every beat.

4:5 · 1080×1350 · 12.8 s · exported in 0.72 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/swiss.json>)

[Video: Endless zoom](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/dive.mp4) 3D rendered by ultrafast

### Endless zoom

One camera falls through seven nested worlds (type, vector blades, a chrome torus, ink, shader contours and rings of type) to the wordmark, whose dot holds the first frame again.

16:9 · 1920×1080 · 28 s · exported in 5.65 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/dive.json>)

[Video: Main titles](https://ultrafast.mov/media/examples/meridian.mp4) 3D rendered by ultrafast

### Main titles

Main titles for a drama that doesn't exist: a brass compass, a sea chart with a route inking itself across it, star trails over the sea, an armillary sphere, and a lighthouse whose beam lights the title.

2.39:1 · 1920×804 · 24 s · exported in 1.44 s · `review`: 0 findings · [document](<https://ultrafast.mov/examples/meridian.json>)
